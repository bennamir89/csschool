SESSION 1

Module 2.1 — System of Projections

45 Minutes

Lesson Objectives (Session 1)

  • Define the basic components of a projection system.
  • Differentiate between Parallel and Perspective projection systems.
  • Identify the three principal planes of projection (HP, VP, PP).

1. ENGAGE 5 mins

Starter Activity: Light & Shadows

Discussion Prompt: How does a 3D object cast a 2D shadow? What happens to the shadow dimensions when light rays are parallel versus when they converge?

2. EXPLORE 10 mins

Perpendicular Line of Sight Inspection

  • Examine a physical 3D block from top, front, and side positions.
  • Observe how depth vanishes when looking strictly perpendicular to each face.

3. EXPLAIN 10 mins

Classification of Projection Systems (Textbook 2.1)

Projection Type Projector Lines Key Characteristics
Perspective Converge at station point Realistic visual representation; non-measurable scale.
Oblique Parallel, at angle to plane Shows front face true shape + inclined depth.
Orthographic Parallel & perpendicular (90°) Exact dimensioning and scale; standard for production.

3. EXPLAIN (Cont.)

Principal Planes of Projection

  • Horizontal Plane (HP): Captures the Top View (Plan).
  • Frontal/Vertical Plane (VP): Captures the Front View (Elevation).
  • Profile Plane (PP): Captures the Side View (Profile).

4. EXTEND 15 mins

In-Class Identification Lab

Classify sample drawings into Perspective, Oblique, or Orthographic views based on projector angles.

5. EVALUATE 5 mins

Exit Ticket (Session 1)

  1. Why are perspective drawings unsuitable for precise manufacturing?
  2. Name the projection plane where the front view is projected.

SESSION 2

Module 2.2 — Orthographic Projections

45 Minutes

Lesson Objectives (Session 2)

  • Apply the "Glass Box" theory to unfold 3D objects into 2D multiview drawings.
  • Differentiate First-Angle and Third-Angle projection standards.
  • Construct aligned views using a 45° miter depth projection line.

1. ENGAGE 5 mins

Global Standard Confusion

Why do US drawings place the Top View above the Front View, while European drawings place it below?


🎥 Watch: 1st Angle vs 3rd Angle Projection

2. EXPLORE 10 mins

Unfolding the Glass Box

  • Project object surfaces onto surrounding glass faces.
  • Unfold the horizontal and profile planes into a single flat plane.

3. EXPLAIN 10 mins

First-Angle vs Third-Angle Standards

Parameter First-Angle Projection (ISO) Third-Angle Projection (ANSI)
Quadrant 1st Quadrant 3rd Quadrant
Order Observer $\rightarrow$ Object $\rightarrow$ Plane Observer $\rightarrow$ Plane $\rightarrow$ Object
Top View Placed BELOW Front View Placed ABOVE Front View

4. EXTEND 15 mins

Multiview Construction Lab

  1. Draw the Front View using width and height dimensions.
  2. Project vertical construction lines up to form the Top View.
  3. Transfer depth to the Right-Side View using a 45° Miter Line.

5. EVALUATE 5 mins

Exit Ticket (Session 2)

  1. Which dimension is shared between Front and Side views?
  2. Draw the standard Third-Angle projection cone symbol.
